Week 1

One flow per promise

The creator ad and the 70% ad each get their own first screens; the “why Buddyfit” ads open the creator flow, and Spain gets its Spanish twin.

Week 2

Judge each ad on what it earns

Each ad’s flow against today’s, measured on trials, paid subscriptions and what each install from that ad earns, so Black Friday spend follows the promise that pays.

Both ads are live in the Meta Ad Library, verbatim above; every one links to buddyfit.club or buddyfit.it, so the two phones are a proposal: what each ad would open if it pointed at the app. The 39,99 € year is a web price we can’t see in the App Store, so the app keeps its own prices and the 70% ad’s flow leads with the 71% the yearly plan really saves against monthly.
